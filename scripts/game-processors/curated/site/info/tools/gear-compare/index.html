How the Gear Compare Calculator Works

1. Expected Damage Index Formula

The main comparison score is not a literal single-hit damage number against a specific boss armor layer; it is the Expected Damage Index used by the source workbook (Everything.xlsx):

Expected Damage = ROUND( Total ATK × (1 + Total DMG%) × (1 + CritDMG% × MIN(1, CritRate)), 0 )

This models your average damage output combining base scaling, all additive damage bonuses, and expected critical hit multiplier.

2. Piece-for-Piece Gear Swapping

When you enter Gear B stats, the solver does not just compare the two pieces in isolation. It takes your character combat snapshot (which is wearing Gear A), subtracts Gear A's stats, adds Gear B's stats, and evaluates your new total combat performance:

  • Base ATK Adjustment: New Base ATK = Old Base ATK - (Gear A Flat + Elem ATK) + (Gear B Flat + Elem ATK)
  • ATK% Adjustment: New ATK% = Old Total ATK% - Gear A ATK% + Gear B ATK%
  • DMG% Adjustment: New DMG% = Old DMG% - Gear A DMG% + Gear B DMG%
  • Crit Adjustment: New Crit Stat = Old Crit Stat - Gear A Crit + Gear B Crit

3. Audit Findings: Plus ATK & DMG% Stacking

Plus ATK Stacking Model: In Tower of Fantasy, character ATK is displayed as Base ATK + Bonus ATK. The workbook treats the ratio Plus ATK / Base ATK as the baseline ATK% multiplier derived from your cactus snapshot. We preserve this workbook parity while making the calculation transparent.
DMG% Additive Stacking: The workbook sums all damage bonus sources additively into M6 (Supercomputer, SSS bonuses, Lin realm, Mia food, and gear DMG%). In some endgame community tests, certain weapon buffs act multiplicatively, but to preserve workbook parity and prevent ungrounded speculation, the verified workbook model is maintained.
Crit Model: Crit rate conversion is computed using the exact level polynomial curve denom(L). We do not inject unproven enemy crit resistance models into gear comparison because gear value is evaluated against standard content baselines.

4. Dynamic Substat Value & Equivalent Flat ATK

The lower table recalculates dynamically from your current combat stats. It computes:

  1. Damage per 1 Flat ATK: Gained by adding 250 flat ATK to your build and dividing the resulting damage gain by 250.
  2. Equivalent Flat ATK (EQ ATK): For any other stat (e.g. +1.44% ATK or +0.72% DMG), EQ ATK = ROUND(Stat Damage Gain / Damage Per ATK).
  3. Roll Quality / Proc Ratio: Compares the EQ ATK to a single flat ATK roll on gold gear using named constants: (EQ ATK - 125) / (312 - 125). A ratio of 100% means the stat provides the exact same value as a maximum possible 312 flat ATK roll!
